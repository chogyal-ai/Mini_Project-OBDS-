About BloodConnect

BloodConnect is a platform designed to bridge the gap between blood donors and those in need.


Our mission is to save lives by making blood donation and request processes simple, efficient, and accessible to everyone.


Key Features:

  • Real-time donor and requester matching
  • Secure messaging between users
  • Location and blood type based search
  • Profile management with photo upload
  • Notification system for new connections
